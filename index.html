Short bio

Lidija Simoliunaite is a Lithuanian founder. She leads Buronia, which helps startups and small companies in Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia and Finland find and win EU and national innovation grants. She started modelling internationally at 14 and took her first web developer job while still in school. She studied software development at HAMK in Finland, helped create the clothing rental marketplace Wardrobers in the University of Helsinki pre-incubator, and built a loyalty app for local restaurants. She has also analysed the financial data behind budget decisions at Enterprise Estonia and worked with real-time production data as a system engineer at Neste.
